# Mission

PeerLens helps researchers improve grant proposals before submission with feedback that is grounded in official call requirements, aligned to evaluation criteria, and traceable enough to trust.

## Interpretation

- Primary user: researchers preparing proposals before submission.
- Core promise: feedback is grounded in official call structure and evaluation criteria, not generic AI commentary.
- Trust standard: outputs should stay precise, traceable, and defensible enough to support real revision decisions.

## Vision

PeerLens aims to make grant review logic legible before submission, so researchers can understand what evaluators expect, improve the parts that matter most, and approach submission with greater clarity and confidence.

## MVP Goal

PeerLens MVP should help a researcher submit a proposal snippet and receive feedback that is clearly tied to the relevant evaluation criteria and traceable to the official call materials.

## Product Goals

- Ground every review card in the official call structure and evaluation criteria.
- Show enough traceability that a researcher can understand why a comment was generated.
- Produce feedback specific enough to support concrete revision decisions before submission.

## MVP Success Criteria

- A researcher can see which evaluation criterion each review card addresses.
- A researcher can inspect the source basis behind the feedback.
- The feedback is actionable enough to help the researcher revise the proposal snippet.

## Working Definitions

- Mission: why PeerLens exists.
- Vision: the longer-term change PeerLens wants to create.
- MVP goal: what the first useful version must achieve.
- Product goals: the specific outcomes the product should accomplish in the current phase.
- Success criteria: how we judge whether the MVP goal and product goals are being met.
